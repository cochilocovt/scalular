# Scalular Design Context

This document outlines the design context, branding guidelines, and aesthetic direction for Scalular, serving as the source of truth for design decisions and automated quality audits.

## Design Context

### Users
- **Target Audience:** Fashion and apparel brands seeking reliable manufacturing, and certified apparel factories looking to grow their client base.
- **Use Cases:**
  - Brands: Discovering pre-vetted factories, getting instant quotes via techpacks, and tracking production from sample to delivery.
  - Factories: Showcasing compliance standards (SEDEX, SLCP, WRAP, GOTS), managing credit flow, and receiving clear tech packs.
- **Evoked Emotions:** Confidence, transparency, professional verification, reliability, and security.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice and Tone:** Premium, verified, trustworthy, and modern.
- **Three-Word Personality:** Verified, Seamless, Premium.
- **Aesthetic Direction:** Elegant light-themed design utilizing a signature warm off-white/beige background (`#E7E3D1`), deep navy brand color (`#171B2E`), clean glassmorphism touches, fluid 3D elements (interactive globe, garment showcase), and clean typography.
- **Anti-References:** Avoid generic "AI-slop" dark-mode neon/purple themes, overused system fonts (plain Arial/Inter), and identical cards or metrics grids without rhythm.

### Design Principles
1. **Material Authenticity & Transparency:** Avoid fake metrics or abstract overlays. Content should be direct, verified, and visually grounded (e.g., actual certifications, direct factory counts).
2. **Asymmetric Grid Rhythm:** Avoid boring, uniform grids. Play with spacing, sizing, and container widths to give sections breathing room.
3. **Intentional Interactivity:** Interactive elements (such as the 3D globe, carousel, and central timeline wheel logo) should guide the user dynamically rather than auto-cycling excessively.
4. **Cohesive Color Harmony:** Respect the warm off-white background and deep navy brand colors; use secondary highlights (like royal blue and subtle sky tints) to indicate primary actions.
